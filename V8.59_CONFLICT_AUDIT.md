# V8.59.4 Conflict Resolution & Audit Trail

Pocket Sync never silently overwrites a conflicting record. Signed-in users review each conflict and choose either **Accept Incoming** or **Keep Existing**. The decision is persisted on the import item and an audit event records the before state, incoming value, resulting state, action, source, and timestamp.

Audit storage is scoped by Supabase RLS to the current Companion user. Credential material is outside the adapter contract and must never be stored in sync metadata.
