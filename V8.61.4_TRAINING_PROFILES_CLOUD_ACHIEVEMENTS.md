# V8.61.4 — Training Profiles + Cloud Stats + Achievements

Adds a unified Training Profile for What’s This Card? and all nine curated Brain Teaser modes. Guest progress remains local. Signed-in users upload completed daily results and puzzle answers to Supabase, restore them across devices, receive aggregate training stats, Training XP/level, and achievement unlocks.

Achievements: First Rep, Brain Starter, Brain Veteran, Sharp Eye, Five-Star Sleuth, Mode Explorer, Complete Competitor, and Perfect Ten.

Cloud tables: `training_daily_card_results`, `training_brain_results`, `training_achievements`. RLS limits rows to the authenticated owner. RPCs: `get_my_training_profile()` and `sync_my_training_achievements()`.

The trainer still never invents card effects; curated puzzle facts remain self-contained.
