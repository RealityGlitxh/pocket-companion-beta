# V8.61.0 — Brain Teasers & Competitive Training Foundation

## Headline mode: What's This Card?
- One deterministic global daily card based on the UTC date and canonical Pocket card dataset.
- Five guesses maximum.
- Artwork begins heavily zoomed and progressively reveals more after wrong guesses.
- Card name/text identifying areas remain masked until the reveal.
- Guess autocomplete accepts only real names from the canonical card database.
- Scoring: 5 stars on guess 1 down to 1 star on guess 5; failure reveals the card.
- Spoiler-free share result.
- Local stats: current streak, best streak, cards solved, average successful guess.
- Guest-safe/localStorage implementation. Cloud/profile stats are intentionally deferred to a V8.61.x follow-up.

## Competitive Training roadmap surfaced in UI
Best Move, Sequencing, KO Math, Energy Management, Retreat Decisions, Matchup Puzzles, and Find the Misplay are the next training modes.

## Grounding rule
The daily game only selects cards from the canonical card database with usable artwork. It does not use AI to invent card identities or effects.
