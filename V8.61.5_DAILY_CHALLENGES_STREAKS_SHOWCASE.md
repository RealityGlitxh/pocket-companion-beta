# V8.61.5 — Daily Challenges + Training Streaks + Profile Showcase

## Daily Challenges
Four deterministic goals refresh with the global daily card: complete the daily card, solve it, solve it in three guesses or fewer, and complete a Brain Teaser that day. The panel shows progress and the XP value earned today.

## Training Streaks
A training day is any UTC day with a completed What’s This Card? result or a Brain Teaser answer. The Training Profile shows current streak, best streak, and total active training days. A missed current day does not break the visible streak until the following day, matching the daily-game grace window.

## Profile Showcase
Players can feature up to three unlocked achievements. Guest selections remain local. Signed-in selections sync to the private `training_profile_showcase` row for that account. The database check constraint prevents more than three featured keys and owner-only RLS prevents another account from editing the showcase.

## Compatibility
V8.61.5 preserves V8.61.4 training result tables, achievement keys, localStorage stores, all 19 curated Brain Teasers, What’s This Card?, canonical `cards.json`, Team Ranked, and Pocket Sync.
