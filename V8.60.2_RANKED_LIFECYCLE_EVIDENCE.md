# V8.60.2 — Ranked Match Lifecycle Hardening + Screenshot Evidence

## Lifecycle rules
- Ranked queue expires after 10 minutes if no match is found.
- A generated private-match code expires after 30 minutes.
- Unsettled matched/reported matches expire after 30 minutes with no RP movement.
- Two players can mutually end a match as no-result. One player requests it; the other must confirm. No RP moves.
- Completed, expired, abandoned, and cancelled matches are removed from the active queue lifecycle.

## Dispute proof requirement
When reciprocal reports conflict, Team RP is frozen and a dispute case is opened. Both actual players must upload at least one screenshot on Pocket Companion before staff can award either team the win.

Evidence rules:
- PNG, JPG/JPEG, or WebP only.
- Maximum 5 MB per screenshot.
- Up to 3 screenshots per player in the frontend.
- Evidence is stored in the private `team-ranked-evidence` Supabase Storage bucket.
- Evidence metadata is stored in `team_ranked_dispute_evidence`.
- Only the two match participants and Team Ranked staff can read the evidence.
- Evidence upload deadline defaults to 24 hours after the dispute opens.
- Staff winner buttons remain locked until both players have submitted proof.
- Staff may void a dispute with no RP change when proof is missing, unclear, unusable, or the case otherwise cannot be fairly settled.
- Existing V8.60.1 resolution audit events remain the permanent record of the final decision.

Do not upload passwords, account credentials, session cookies, private tokens, or unrelated personal information as dispute evidence.

## Beta QA flow
V8.60.2 adds a staff-visible manual QA checklist covering two separate accounts/teams, matchmaking, same-tag blocking, reciprocal settlement, dispute freeze, both-player screenshot evidence, staff resolution, and lifecycle timeout/no-result behavior.
