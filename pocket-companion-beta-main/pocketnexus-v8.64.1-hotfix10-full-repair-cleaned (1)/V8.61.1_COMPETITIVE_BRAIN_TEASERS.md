# V8.61.1 — Competitive Brain Teasers

Adds three playable training modes to the V8.61 Training hub: Best Move, Find the Misplay, and KO Math.

## Grounding rule
V8.61.1 uses curated board-state facts and arithmetic instead of inventing card effects. A future effect-aware puzzle may only become playable after every referenced effect is resolved from the canonical card database.

## Progress
Answers, correctness, completion count, and accuracy are stored locally in this foundation build. Existing What's This Card? progress remains unchanged.
