Help & Support

For problems, first reopen PocketNexus and use More → Advanced & troubleshooting → Environment Tests. You can also open About & Privacy and create a diagnostic report to review before sharing.

Account or cloud problem

Check Account & Cloud, confirm you are signed into the expected account, and avoid repeatedly creating duplicate accounts while troubleshooting.

Missing local data

Do not clear site data while troubleshooting. If you have a PocketNexus backup, use the Restore Backup action in More.

Competitive result issue

For Team Ranked disputes, use the built-in evidence and dispute workflow. Do not include passwords, authentication tokens, or unrelated private information in screenshots.

Security

Never send passwords, provider secrets, session cookies, or private API keys in a support report. PocketNexus will never require your Nintendo/Pokémon password.

Contact destination

A permanent public support email, form, or community support destination has not been configured in this build. Configure one before broad public launch.
