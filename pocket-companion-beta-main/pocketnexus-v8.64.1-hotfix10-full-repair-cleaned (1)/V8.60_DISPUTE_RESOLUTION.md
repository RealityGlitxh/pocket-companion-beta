# V8.60.1 Team Ranked Dispute Resolution

## Resolution outcomes
- `team_a_win`: settle Team A as winner using the match's fixed rating delta.
- `team_b_win`: settle Team B as winner using the match's fixed rating delta.
- `void`: cancel the disputed match and move no Team RP.

## Safety
Resolution is restricted to explicitly authorized rows in `team_ranked_staff`. The browser cannot create those rows through RLS. Settlement runs in one database transaction and refuses a second settlement if rating history already exists.

## Audit
Every staff resolution writes a `team_ranked_dispute_events` entry containing the dispute, match, acting staff account, outcome, optional note, timestamp, and the pre-resolution match snapshot.
