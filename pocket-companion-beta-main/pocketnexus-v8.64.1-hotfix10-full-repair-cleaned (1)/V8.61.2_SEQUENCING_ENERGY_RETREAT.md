# V8.61.2 — Sequencing + Energy Management + Retreat Decisions

V8.61.2 expands Competitive Brain Teasers from three to six playable modes.

## Added
- Sequencing
- Energy Management
- Retreat Decisions

The new puzzles are curated board-state reasoning exercises. They deliberately state every fact needed to solve the scenario and do not claim or invent live card effects. Card-specific effect puzzles remain gated on canonical card-data verification.

Progress remains local and compatible with V8.61.1's Brain Teaser storage so existing answers are preserved after upgrading.
