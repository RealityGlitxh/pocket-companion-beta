# V8.60.4 — Final Team Ranked Polish + Beta Fixes

V8.60.4 is the release-candidate polish pass for Team Ranked. It intentionally avoids changing the ranked rating model or dispute backend.

## Final polish
- Confirm Win/Loss before sending a ranked report.
- Clipboard fallback for private match codes.
- 15-second foreground refresh while an active queue or match is open.
- Visibility/reconnect refresh when the player returns to the Ranked tab.
- Final staff QA checklist with explicit PASS state and reset control.
- Mobile result controls and focus accessibility polish.

## Completion gate
V8.60 is only considered complete after a hosted test with two separate accounts on different teams/tags passes every item in the in-app V8.60.4 QA checklist. The test must cover matchmaking, reconnect recovery, +20/-20 settlement, duplicate/same-team/tag protections, disputes, screenshot evidence from both players, staff resolution/void, timeout/no-result, and leaderboard/history/contributor updates.

No Supabase DDL is introduced by this version.
