Privacy

Last updated September 3, 2026

What PocketNexus is

PocketNexus is an independent third-party companion for Pokémon TCG Pocket. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by The Pokémon Company, Nintendo, Creatures, or Limitless.

Guest data

Guest-mode data is stored in your browser on the device you use. Clearing browser/site storage can remove that local data, so use the app's backup tools for information you want to keep.

Signed-in data

When you choose to sign in, supported account, profile, deck, collection, battle, rank, team, training, achievement, simulation, preference, and sync records may be stored in PocketNexus's cloud backend so supported features can work across devices.

Public information

Profile information, team information, achievements, leaderboards, or competitive information that you intentionally publish or expose through public features may be visible to other users. Review privacy and profile controls before sharing information.

Third-party services

The app may use third-party infrastructure and public/community data sources for authentication, cloud storage, card information, tournament/meta information, and AI-assisted features. Those services may process technical requests needed to provide their function.

Credentials

PocketNexus does not need your Nintendo or Pokémon password. Never enter Nintendo, Pokémon, Google, Apple, or other account passwords except in the official authentication flow for that provider.

Diagnostics and feedback

Diagnostic reports are created locally for you to review before sharing. They can include app version, current page, online status, browser user-agent information, and pass/fail diagnostic results. They are designed not to include passwords or private service keys.

Contact and changes

This launch-candidate privacy notice describes the behavior implemented in this build. Before a broad public launch, the project owner should publish a stable support/contact destination and review this notice for the laws and audiences that apply to the service.
